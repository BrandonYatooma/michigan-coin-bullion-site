How do I get a quote? +

Call (248) 720-4092 or send photos and a description of your items. We'll set up an in-person evaluation so we can give you an accurate, no-obligation offer.

Do I need an appointment? +

No — walk-ins are always welcome during business hours, no appointment necessary. If you're bringing in a large collection, you're welcome to call ahead so we can plan accordingly, but it's never required.

Do you buy full estate or inherited collections? +

Yes. We regularly evaluate full estate and inherited collections of coins, bullion, and jewelry, and are happy to work with executors and family members.

Is there any obligation once I get an offer? +

No. Evaluations are free and come with no obligation to sell — take whatever time you need to decide.
